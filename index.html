What is LAYR?

LAYR is a speculative, retromodern hardware brand concept developed as a final year Creative Computing project. It explores how physical product design, digital fabrication, and visual communication can operate as a cohesive system rather than isolated outputs.

What was explored

  • Manufacturing‑aware enclosure design using parametric CAD
  • Consumer‑grade FDM 3D printing as a creative constraint
  • Material behaviour, tolerances, and fastening strategies
  • Digital representation through animation and web presentation

What was produced

  • Parametric CAD models of a physical product enclosure
  • 3D‑printed PETG prototypes
  • A Blender‑based animated product showcase
  • A web‑based digital artefact contextualising the project

Why this matters

The project prioritises clarity of intent, manufacturability, and design‑led reasoning over full technical implementation. It demonstrates how small‑scale physical products can be developed responsibly within realistic constraints while maintaining strong visual communication.
